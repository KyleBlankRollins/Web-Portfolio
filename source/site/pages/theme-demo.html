Theme Features

🌓 Light & Dark Modes

Every theme includes carefully crafted light and dark variants with proper contrast ratios for accessibility.

♿ Accessibility First

All themes meet WCAG AA standards with support for reduced motion and high contrast preferences.

💾 Persistent Preferences

Your theme and color scheme choices are saved and restored across sessions using localStorage.
