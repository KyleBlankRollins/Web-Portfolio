## Boundary checklist

- Define your protected work window and write it down.[^deep-work]
- Decide what counts as an emergency interruption.
- Choose your visible signal for focus mode (for example, a closed door or headphones).
- Agree on one low-friction communication path for non-urgent questions.
- Schedule two daily check-in windows so household communication still has a place.
- Track context-switches for one week and adjust your boundaries based on what you learn.[^attention-residue]
