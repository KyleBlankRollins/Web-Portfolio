## Senior Technical Writer

As part of a docs team, I used a docs-as-code approach to contribute to the [Netlify docs](https://docs.netlify.com/). I used the same tools that developers do so that I could be closer to their work and they could more easily contribute to mine.

When I started at Netlify, I covered the core build system and integrations, like build plugins. Later, I documented Netlify's ecosystem integrations. This means I spent a lot of time in the wider web development community and figuring out how various tools and frameworks do their magic on Netlify.
