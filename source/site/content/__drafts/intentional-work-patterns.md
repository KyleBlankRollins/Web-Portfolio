# Intentional Work Patterns

This is a series of posts that highlight work patterns I've learned throughout my career. Most of these focus on remote work, but many are also applicable to office settings.

1. Setting boundaries
2. Remote work traps

- Just another 5 minutes
- I don't need to take a long lunch - don't need breaks
- I did a few chores, I should work longer to make up for it
  - Bullshit. People often do inconsequential stuff in the office that wastes time.

3. Work start and work end rituals

- Enforce work life and personal life boundaries

4. Physical spaces

- If possible, find a dedicated work space in your home
- Make it a space that's comfortable and encourages focus

5. Intentionality

- Be the one to reach out. Remote relationships require more work and effort. They don't just happen. Ask for colleagues to introduce you to other people in the company.
- Schedule dedicated social time with teams and individuals
- Schedule focus time on your calendar
